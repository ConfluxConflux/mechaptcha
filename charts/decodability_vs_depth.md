Each point is the mean test accuracy of a logistic-regression probe trained to classify distorted (batch A) vs. undistorted (batch B) images from the layer's activations, averaged over 11 distortion types (pixel-level noise: blur, dots, salt & pepper; geometric: rotation, wave, wavy line, horizontal line, angled line, two lines; font style: bold, italic). Random-label controls confirmed that probes find genuine signal rather than overfitting (not shown; ≈50% at all layers and all models). Models: a CNN trained from scratch on the CAPTCHA transcription task; DINOv2-S, DINOv2-B (self-supervised), and CLIP-B (language-supervised vision tower), each pretrained on ImageNet-scale data and then LoRA-adapted on the transcription task to establish behavioral invariance to the distortions before probing.
